# ResQ3D Development Phases

Phase 1 — Digital Twin: current folder.
Phase 2 — Emergency Simulation: current folder.
Phase 3 — IoT integration: ESP32 + smoke/temperature/flame sensors.
Phase 4 — Backend/API: receive live sensor events and control simulation.
Phase 5 — Database: store rooms, sensors, alerts, incidents and event history.
Phase 6 — AI: camera/sensor-based fire/smoke detection and risk analysis.
Phase 7 — Intelligent evacuation: calculate safe route based on fire/danger zones.
Phase 8 — Alerts: dashboard/browser/buzzer and optional messaging.
Phase 9 — Testing & evaluation: normal operation, fire on each floor, blocked exit, high occupancy, sensor-triggered fire, AI-triggered fire.
Phase 10 — Documentation: black book, architecture, flowcharts, test cases, screenshots and results.


## Phase 2B — Intelligent Evacuation
- Select a fire floor and room.
- Mark the incident room and nearby affected rooms.
- Select a safe staircase based on the fire side.
- Generate a route from the incident room to the assembly point.
- Replace placeholder evacuation markers with lightweight low-poly human models.
- Animate arms and legs while people move.
- Track people reaching the assembly point.

## Phase 4
AI Emergency Risk Analysis: live risk score, risk level, trend-aware inference and recommendations.
